Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What data the app uses

  • TikTok access tokens and your TikTok display name, received when you log in with TikTok. They are needed to upload videos to your account.
  • Public information about your YouTube channel: video IDs, titles and descriptions from the channel's public feed, and the video files themselves.

Where the data is stored

All data is stored only on your own computer, in the app's settings folder. The app has no server, does not collect analytics and does not send your data to anyone except TikTok (to upload your videos) and YouTube (to read your public videos).

Sharing

Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time by deleting the app's settings folder (%APPDATA%\TyzReposter) and revoking the app's access in your TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions).

Contact
